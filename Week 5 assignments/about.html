Preparing For A Cruise

Cruises are my favorite type of vactions. It's a very fun way to travel you get to go to many destinations in one week. It is important to know what you need to bring on a cruise.

Some important things to pack include clothes, swimwear, dress clothes, sunscreen. You also have to bring the right travel documents like a passport and Photo ID.

Things to Do on a Cruise

Cruise ships offer activities for all ages. You will see swimming pools, arcades, and good shows. My favorite activities to do on the ship is swimming and playing basketball.

Cruises also stop in all types of places. At these places you can try new food, explore the place, or shop on the port. My favorite destination is the Bahamas and The Dominican Republic.

Best Criuse Lines

There are a lot of great cruise lines in the cruise industry. My top 3 favorites are Royal Caribbean, MSC, and Carnival. Royal Caribbean has the best shows out of the three. MSC is a more laid back curise still with a lot to do and has very good pizaa. Carnival is more geared towards young people and families. Carnival is the party ship as it is known as the Fun Ships.
